Supreme Court Audit Protocol
Layer 1 (Detective): Clone the peer repo, read the rubric.json, and parse the PDF. Output an evidence_log.json.

Layer 2 (Justice): Spawn two sub-agents: Prosecutor (finds flaws) and Defense (finds justifications). They must debate the evidence_log.json.

Layer 3 (Chief): Synthesize the debate into a final score and write audit_report.md to the /reports folder.
